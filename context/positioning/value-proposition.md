# Rave — Value Proposition

## What they actually buy

Proof, a finished asset, and zero risk, wrapped around **more booked jobs from people already
finding them on Google**.

## Value equation (Hormozi frame, as used in the SCOPE method)

| Lever | Rave |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | More calls and booked jobs from the same Google traffic |
| Perceived likelihood | They see the finished design with their name, city and a real review before paying |
| Time delay | Live by next week |
| Effort and sacrifice | One 10-20 minute call; Rave handles build, hosting, changes |

## Offer

- **$2,000 one-time build.** One price, no tiers (tiers turn a short call into a comparison exercise).
- **$97/mo care plan:** hosting, maintenance, any changes. Cancel whenever. Pitched as included, not an upsell.
- **Risk reversal:** "If you like it and want it up by next week, I'll set it up and pay for it myself."

Why $2,000 and not less (decided 2026-08-11): job values are $2k-$30k+, so $1,500 vs $2,000 does
not change the decision; cheap reads as a scam; the anchor being beaten is $5-10k over months.

## Lead magnet

The sample redesign for their trade, customized with their business name, city, phone and one of
their real Google reviews, shown only after they reply. One design per trade keeps the cost flat
no matter how many owners are emailed.
