# Rave — Positioning

Evidence: `context/docs/research/competitors.md` (checked 2026-11-13; labels there).

## Statement

For **owner-run local trades** whose Google reviews are better than their website, **Rave** is
**a one-person website builder** that **shows you a finished design for your trade before you pay
anything, and has the real site live within a week**. Unlike trade marketing agencies, there is
no 12-month contract, no hidden monthly price, and no account manager: you deal with the person
who builds it.

## Category

"Website rebuild for local trades." Not "digital marketing", not "SEO agency", not "lead gen".
Owners already distrust those categories (see customer voice research).

## The alternatives the owner actually weighs

| Alternative | What they get | Where it hurts them | Rave's answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do nothing (referrals + Google Maps) | Free | Searchers who check the site and bounce are invisible | "Not broken, but losing you jobs" |
| Trade marketing agency (Scorpion, Hibu, Thryv, Roofing Webmasters) | Site + bundled marketing | Quote-only or monthly pricing, contracts (Scorpion's FAQ: 12 months, per a search snippet, **Unverified**), cancellation and billing complaints (Hibu BBB, **Confirmed**) | One price, cancel the care plan any time, see it first |
| DIY builder (Wix, Squarespace, ~$12-40/mo, **Confirmed**) | Cheap | Owner has no time; looks templated | Done for you in a week |
| Field-service software add-on (Housecall Pro, Jobber) | Site bundled with ops software | Generic template | Built around how homeowners choose a contractor |
| Subscription site builders for trades (Rebolt: $0 setup, $225-300/mo, **Confirmed** on its page) | Fast, no contract | Ongoing high monthly cost | Lower total cost after about 10-16 months ($2,000 ÷ the $128-203/mo difference), and they see the design before saying yes |
| Google Business Profile website | Was free | **Shut down 2024-03-01 (Confirmed)** | A real site that keeps the reviews front and center |

## Where Rave wins

1. **Show before pay.** None of the competitor pages checked offered a finished sample before purchase.
2. **No lock-in.** One build price; the $97/mo care plan cancels any time.
3. **Speed.** Days, not the ~4 weeks one trade agency advertises.
4. **A person, not an agency.** Answers objection #4 ("I got burned before").

## Where Rave is weak (be honest about it)

- $2,000 upfront vs Rebolt's $0 setup. Answer: total cost, and seeing it first.
- One-person capacity caps volume. Fine at the goal of 5 deals in 90 days.
- No owned case studies yet (`context/docs/case-studies.md`).
- A sample built per trade can look templated. The personal touches (name, city, their real review) must carry it.
