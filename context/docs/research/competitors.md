# Rave GTM: Competitive Landscape (website redesigns for US local trades)

Checked 2026-11-13. Prices move; re-check before quoting any number to a prospect.

## Summary (5 lines)
1. Agencies (Scorpion, Hibu, Thryv, restoration/roofing shops) mostly hide prices; where shown, entry is "$99 to start" (Roofing Webmasters, Thryv) plus a monthly fee, or $750 to $5,000+/mo retainers, and owner complaints centre on contracts, cancellation and templated sites.
2. DIY builders are cheap ($12 to $40/mo) but the owner does the work; the owner's real competitor is "my nephew / Wix / my old site", not another agency.
3. Lead platforms (Angi, Thumbtack) sell leads, not sites; Housecall Pro and Jobber bundle a site as a side feature; Google killed its free Business Profile website on 2024-03-01.
4. A direct low-cost rival exists: Rebolt claims $0 setup, $225 to $300/mo, no contract, live in 1 to 3 days (their own page, unverified delivery).
5. Rave's open gap is "see the finished sample free before paying, owner-run, no lock-in, flat $2,000 + $97/mo"; no competitor page checked offered show-before-pay. Caveat: unproven brand, and `$97/mo` is cheaper than rivals but `$2,000` upfront is not.

Labels: **Confirmed** = source fetched this session (URL given). **Likely** = recollection, no source. **Unverified** = stated by a source I could not check or by a competitor's marketing; verify step given.

## 1. Landscape by category

### A. Agency / managed-marketing vendors

**Scorpion** (scorpion.co)

Unverified; verify by requesting a quote or a past client's invoice. Source: https://rebolthq.com/alternatives/scorpion-alternatives (fetched 2026-11-13).
- Contract: Scorpion's own FAQ says it "typically requires a 12-month contract" for marketing technology and services like SEO. Confirmed via search snippet of https://www.scorpion.co/faqs/ (the page itself returned 403 to my fetch, so treat as Likely-to-Confirmed; re-read the page by browser).
- Ownership: FAQ says on cancellation the team provides "static files, design assets, and content" (same source, same caveat). Rebolt claims the site sits on proprietary CMS-8 and cannot be taken (Unverified, competitor claim).
- Complaints: one BBB complaint says "ALL I bought was a website" and the site would be removed after 30-day notice; BBB lists 2 complaints in 3 years (Likely, from search summary of https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/valencia/profile/digital-marketing/scorpion-llc-1216-13207426/complaints; page not fetched). Mixed Trustpilot reviews (https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/scorpion.co, not fetched).
- Delivery time: not found. Unverified.

**Hibu**
- Price: Hibu's own pages were not read (https://www.hibu.com/websites returned 403). No Hibu price confirmed; a comparison site says it varies and reportedly requires 12 months (Unverified; verify with a quote or browser read).
- Contract: BBB complaints say some customers were bound to a second, longer commitment or a term beyond the six months expected (second longer commitment, term beyond the six months expected). Confirmed: https://www.bbb.org/us/ia/cedar-rapids/profile/digital-marketing/hibu-inc-0664-32057924/complaints shows 107 complaints in 3 years, 38 in last 12 months (as of fetch).

### E. Google Business Profile website
- Confirmed gone: Google shut the free "websites made with Business Profiles" on 2024-03-01, redirecting to the listing until 2024-06-10. Source: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-shutting-down-google-business-profiles-websites-36686.html. ## 3. Where Rave can win

1. **Show-before-pay.** Of the pages I fetched (Thryv, Roofing Webmasters, Rebolt, restoration shop list, Wix, Squarespace), none offers a finished, trade-specific sample shown free before any payment. Roofing Webmasters offers a risk-free refund trial (Confirmed, its page), which is pay-then-refund, a weaker answer to "sounds like a scam". Best answer to the scam and "got burned" objections.
2. **No lock-in.** Hibu's complaint record is dominated by cancellation and contract disputes (Confirmed, BBB link above); Scorpion states 12-month terms (own FAQ). A written no-contract, cancel-anytime, you-own-the-site promise speaks directly to this. Note Rebolt also claims no contract, so this alone is not unique.
3. **Speed.** Roofing Webmasters says about four weeks (Confirmed, its page); "up by next week" is faster. Rebolt claims faster (24 hours to 3 days, self-contradicting), so speed is a parity point against them, a win against agencies.
4. **Price vs expensive agencies.** $2,000 + $97/mo is far under the $2,500 to $10,000 custom-roofing-site range cited (Unverified) and the $750 to $5,000+/mo retainers; the answer to "web people are expensive".
5. **Solo builder who answers.** Hibu complaints include owners who could not get through by phone (Confirmed, BBB). A named person with a direct line addresses this. Evidence is about Hibu only.
6. **Trigger: Google Business Profile site shutdown** (Confirmed) leaves some owners with a dead or redirected site.

## 4. Caveats (arguing against section 3)

- **Show-before-pay may not be unique.** I only checked a small set of pages and did not browse ad libraries, Facebook groups or the many small "free mockup" web shops; offering a free mockup is a common freelancer tactic (Likely). Verify by searching "free website mockup contractors".
- **Rebolt undercuts Rave on monthly price** ($225 to $300 vs $97 is lower for Rave, but Rebolt has $0 setup vs Rave's $2,000). For a cash-tight owner, $0 setup may beat $2,000 upfront. Unverified whether Rebolt delivers or how its sites look.
- **"No contract" and "owner keeps site" are claimed by Rebolt and Hibu (marketing claims)**, so they differentiate only if Rave puts them in writing and the owner believes them.
- **One-person capacity risk.** "Up by next week" and answering the phone do not scale; a rival's four-week delivery (Roofing Webmasters) do not prove Rave stays fast at volume. Success of the cold-email play could break the promise.
- **A sample is per trade, not per owner.** A generic sample may look templated, the exact complaint owners cite about Hibu (Confirmed, BBB). Customizing only after a reply is the mitigation.
- **"My site is fine, I get referrals"** is not addressed by any competitor comparison; the real competitor is inertia, and no source here measures how many owners have a working site.
- **Much negative evidence is competitor marketing** (Rebolt, cc-marketing, Webtonic) or search summaries; only the Hibu BBB page, Thryv, Wix, Squarespace, Housecall Pro, Jobber, Rebolt's own page and the Google shutdown were fetched directly. Scorpion, Hibu and Thryv site fetches failed or were partial (403s).
- **Not researched:** Yelp, ServiceTitan, individual local freelancers, Fiverr/Upwork actual prices, GoDaddy renewal prices, per-trade shops beyond restoration and roofing (foundation, moving, tree, fencing, paving). Next step: manual browser pass on those.
