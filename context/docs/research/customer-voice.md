# Customer voice: how local trade owners talk about websites, web providers, Google leads and cold email

Researched 2026-11-13 with free web search and fetch only. Written for Rave GTM (US owner-run trades, $2,000 build + $97/mo care plan, free sample redesign pitch).

Label key: **Confirmed** = page fetched this session and the text appeared in the fetch result (URL given). **Likely** = consistent with several sources but not checked directly. **Unverified** = seen only in a search snippet or a vendor claim.

## 1. Summary

1. **Reddit could not be read.** reddit.com, old.reddit.com and the Reddit mirrors were blocked or down; ContractorTalk and RoofingTalk redirect to a paywall (HTTP 402) that I did not pay. Confirmed.
2. Verbatim owner voice below comes from a window-cleaner forum thread (2018), a pressure-washing forum, and Trustpilot reviews of Thryv, Hibu and Scorpion (contractor reviewers, mostly HVAC). The trades Rave targets (roofing, restoration, moving, tree, paving) are mostly NOT directly represented. This is a thin, adjacent sample.
3. Strongest pattern: owners who got burned describe being locked in (contracts, cancellation fees, losing the site), paying for leads or sites that did nothing, and not owning what they paid for.
4. Strongest skeptic pattern: "all my new work is referrals", "unsolicited text or email means scam", and an opposing voice that says referrals look you up on Google and reviews decide.
5. Messaging implication: lead with ownership, no lock-in, proof before payment, and a real local reviewer quote. Treat everything here as hypotheses to test on real replies, not as settled customer truth.

## 3. Objections to buying a new site or replying to cold outreach

**3.1 "Unsolicited message means scam" (pressure-washing owners, about fake quote requests, not web pitches)**
- "Ignore all texts and emails. If it's important they'll call you." https://pressurewashingresource.com/community/t/scam-i-need-quote-for-all-services/35104 Confirmed (context: fake customer inquiries, so it shows scam reflex toward unknown messages, not toward web pitches specifically)
- "Last couple times I tried to schedule an in person meeting and usually they disappear." same URL, Confirmed
- Counter-voice: "That's terrible advice lol. I'd be broke and out on the street." same URL, Confirmed. Owners do not all ignore unknown messages, because inquiries are income.

**3.2 "Another web or SEO cold email"**
- A r/smallbusiness thread titled "Can we stop with the cold emailing offering SEO" (Apr 2024) exists; a search summary says commenters suggest asking senders for their own homepage because they often have none. ## 4. What owners say drives jobs, and how they judge a site

- "Typically it's referrals, previous customers, Angie's List, Nextdoor or Google searches." https://community.windowcleaner.com/t/solo-guy-not-seeing-need-for-website-anymore/48931 Confirmed
- "More people are just going by Google reviews now." (same thread) Confirmed
- "your 'listing' on the internet is more important than your ad was in the yellow pages." (same thread) Confirmed
- "But I suppose that could be accomplished with a free google business page." (same thread) Confirmed. Some see the Google profile as a replacement for a site.
- "I think we added 300 new customers this year alone from our website." (same thread) Confirmed (self-reported, Unverified as a number)
- "I have a website, a really nice website too. I rank #1, I have the most 5 star reviews on google and facebook." (pressure washer) https://pressurewashingresource.com/community/t/door-to-door-works/8506 Confirmed. Rank and review count are how this owner scores his site.
- "Thank goodness for HomeAdvisor and Facebook swap & shops." (same thread) Confirmed
- "I have the ability to edit my website whenever and I can keep track of leads" (Hibu reviewer, a positive) https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hibu.com Confirmed. Editability and lead tracking are valued.

How they judge a site (inferred from the above, Likely): by calls and leads received, by Google rank and reviews, by whether the info is right, and by whether they can change it. No owner here described judging by "looks".

Vendor claims (Unverified, sellers have a stake): Google Maps drives "almost 50% of online engagement" with local roofers; aim for 4.5 stars and 50+ reviews (from marketing blogs surfaced by search; not opened).

## 6. Implications for Rave messaging

Each is a hypothesis tied to the evidence above.

1. **State ownership plainly: domain and site are yours, leave anytime, and say what happens if you cancel the $97.** Evidence: 2.2 (lose the site on exit, hosting taken away). Risk: the $97/mo care plan is the same shape owners resent; say what you keep without paying.
2. **No contract, no auto-upsell, in one line.** Evidence: 2.3 (6-month lock-in from upsell, cancellation talk-outs).
3. **Show the finished sample first (already the offer).** Evidence: 3.1 ("they disappear", scam reflex) and the Reddit snippet idea of asking for a homepage. A concrete sample with their name plus a real review is a legitimacy proof. Unverified that it converts.
4. **Use their own Google review as the proof element.** Evidence: reviews are how owners and customers judge ("5 star reviews", "going by Google reviews", section 4).
5. **Answer "I get all my work from referrals" with the lookup behavior, not a lecture.** Evidence: "Your referrals may be looking you up before they call to see if you're legit." (3.3). Keep it one sentence.
6. **Measure in calls, not design.** Evidence: owners judge by "leads", "calls" (2.1, 4). Offer a tracking phone number or call count if feasible; "keep track of leads" was praised.
7. **Say "solo builder, one person, you can text me".** Evidence: pains are mostly with large providers (Thryv, Hibu, Scorpion). A named individual contrasts with that. Likely, not tested.
8. **Do not promise ranking or lead volume.** Evidence: reviewers punished "no leads" and wasted spend (2.1). A redesign alone may not move leads.
9. **Keep email short and plain, no agency vocabulary** (section 5).

## 7. Caveats and counter-arguments

- **Source gap (main weakness).** Reddit, ContractorTalk and RoofingTalk were not readable. I have zero verbatim quotes from roofers, restoration, moving, tree, fencing or paving owners. Window cleaning, pressure washing and HVAC are adjacent, not the target. Confirmed.
- **Selection bias.** Trustpilot reviewers are mostly unhappy and mostly customers of big, contract-heavy vendors. Rave's cold-email recipients are not those people yet, and a solo builder with a free sample may face far less lock-in anxiety than my quotes suggest. Reddit and forums also skew to vocal owners, so quiet owners who just ignore email are invisible here.
- **Old data.** The key forum thread is from 2018-12-25 (Confirmed date). Google and AI search behavior has shifted since; treat as dated.
- **Tool limits.** Quotes were extracted by the fetch tool's summarizer, not by me reading raw pages. I used them as returned; a spot check of any quote before it goes into live copy is wise. Trustpilot reviews were fetched without dates.
- **Argument against my own conclusion 1 (ownership first).** Few owners in this sample said "domain" at all. The domain-ownership story comes mainly from a trade-body author and vendor blogs that sell "owned asset" services (Hook Agency, Oneupweb and others appeared in results). Owners may care more about "can I leave and keep my site" than about the word "domain". Test the phrasing.
- **Argument against my own conclusion 3 (free sample builds trust).** Unsolicited messages with a link or attachment are exactly what some owners call scams ("Ignore all texts and emails"). A sample may raise suspicion in some, and a sample with their name plus a real review can feel like surveillance. Test it.
- **Referral-heavy owners may be right.** A solo window cleaner added "4 or 5 new customers each year" by referral and cancelled his domain; for some of Rave's targets a $2,000 site may genuinely not pay back. Emergency trades (water damage, roofing leaks, towing-like moving jobs) are more search-driven, but that is Likely, not shown here.
- **Not checked at all:** Rave-specific reply data, actual cold-email reaction from trade owners to web pitches, Reddit sentiment, LSA (Local Services Ads) talk, Yelp/Google reviews of trade web agencies.
