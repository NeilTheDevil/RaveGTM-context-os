## Proof Rave CAN use

- **Demonstration.** A finished sample design per trade at a live link. The owner judges it in
  seconds without trusting a claim. This is the main proof.
- **Specificity.** Quoting one of their own real Google reviews proves Rave actually looked.
- **Neil's experience, worded honestly:** "I've worked on campaigns like this before" /
  "Most of my work's been with trades." (see `context/messaging/facts.md`)

## Plan to own proof

Build 3 real sites free or near-free for businesses that can be named. Agree up front:
1. Written permission to use their name and site.
2. A short testimonial.
3. Before-and-after numbers: calls and direction requests from their Google Business Profile,
   form submissions, quote requests. Record the baseline before launch, measure at 30 days.
