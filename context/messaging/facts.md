## Banned

### Banned angles
- Borrowed results presented as Rave's.
- Claiming a per-company mockup already exists before they reply.
- Diagnosing the owner as failing. Flatter the work, indict the site.
- Promises Rave can't fulfil yet (e.g. "I'll send you 5 homeowners" needs a fulfilment plan, open item 5).
- Specific meeting times that are not in the Calendly (open item 1).

### Banned words (Rave-specific; global list in `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml`)
- "digital presence", "online presence", "conversion rate", "UX", "SEO-optimized", "agency" (about Rave), "revolutionize", "elevate"

### Never in copy
- Names of other agency clients, prospect names from earlier campaigns, sending infrastructure.

## Voice notes

- Register: plain, casual, owner to owner, lowercase-friendly.
- House phrases already live: "Had a look at your website", "Mind if I show you", "quick 10 minute zoom".
- Subjects: 2-4 words, read like a customer wrote them: "water damage job", "roof quote", "{{reviewer_name}}'s review".

## CTA ladder

- **Primary:** "Mind if I show you what it'd look like for {{company_name}}?"
- **Secondary:** a quick 10 minute call to walk through it.
- Never: a calendar link in email 1.

## Internal only (never in prospect copy)

Pricing, lead counts, reply rates, cost per lead, sending setup, the money model.
