# Rave — Messaging

Evidence: `context/docs/research/customer-voice.md` (thin, adjacent-trade sample; see its caveats),
`context/docs/research/competitors.md`, and the brief digest in `context/docs/source/`.
Claims in copy come only from `facts.md`.

## Core message

**Your work is better than your website. Let me show you what it looks like when the site
matches the reviews, before you spend a dollar.**

## Pillars

| Pillar | What we say | Proof we can show |
|---|---|---|
| 1. See it first | "Costs you nothing to look." | The finished sample for their trade, with their name, city and a real review |
| 2. Fast | "Up by next week." | Delivery in days vs ~4 weeks advertised by one trade agency (competitors research) |
| 3. No lock-in | One build price; care plan cancels any time | Contrast with agency contracts and billing-after-cancel complaints (Hibu BBB, Confirmed) |
| 4. One person | "It's just me. You'd be dealing with me directly." | Real name, answers own email |
| 5. Calls, not looks | Built around turning a Google search into a phone call | Owners judge a site by calls and jobs, not design (customer voice) |

## Messaging by awareness

| Owner thinks | Lead with |
|---|---|
| "My site is fine, I get referrals" | Referrals look you up before they call (customer voice). Not broken, losing jobs |
| "Web people burned me" | No contract, one price, see it first |
| "Sounds like a scam" | Their own review quoted back, a real name, a live link, a short call |
| "Too busy" | One 10-minute look, everything handled |

## Proposed claims (NOT approved, not in facts.md)

These came out of the research. Neil must confirm they are true before they enter `facts.md`:

- "You own the site and the domain. If you cancel the care plan, it's still yours." Owners' top
  fear is losing the site on exit (customer voice). Only usable if Rave's terms actually say so.
- "No contract." True for the build per the offer; confirm wording for the care plan.

## Words to use / avoid

Use: jobs, calls, homeowners, Google, reviews, quote, booked, "people finding you on Google".
Avoid: SEO, UX, redesign (vendor word per customer voice; note the live copy uses "improvements"),
conversion, digital presence, agency (about Rave), leverage, elevate.
