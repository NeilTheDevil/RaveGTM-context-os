## Tone of voice

- **Plain.** Words a roofer uses on a job site. No marketing vocabulary.
- **Short.** One idea per email, under 90 words, readable on a phone between jobs.
- **Specific.** Their city, their company name, one of their real reviews.
- **Generous.** Something free, nothing owed.
- **Respectful.** The owner is clearly good at the work; the site is what is underselling it.

## Key phrases

- "Not broken, but losing you jobs."
- "You're clearly great at what you do. The site just isn't showing it."
- "Costs you nothing to look."
- "Up by next week."
- "It's just me."

## Words we avoid

See `context/messaging/facts.md` → Banned. Also no em dashes in prospect copy.
